Work in a practical, engineering-first way.

For complex or ambiguous tasks, plan before coding.
Default to safe, non-destructive actions.
Ask before destructive changes, broad refactors, or actions outside the current scope.
Be concise, explicit, and honest about assumptions, risks, and unknowns.
Prefer deterministic solutions before adding agentic complexity.

Project-specific memory map rules:
- The Barbados island outline must use an accurate geodata-derived shape, not a hand-drawn approximation.
- The landing map must prioritize a large, clear landmass with readable, non-overlapping labelled pins.
- When multiple stories belong to the same central area, keep the story association accurate but spread the visible pin labels enough for children to tap and read confidently.

When reporting progress, always state:

* what changed
* what was verified
* what assumptions remain
* the next recommended step
