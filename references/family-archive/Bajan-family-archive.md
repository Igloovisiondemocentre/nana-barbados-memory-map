## How to read this archive

- `High confidence`: clearly readable from images or repeated across multiple sources
- `Medium confidence`: likely correct, but partly affected by blur, cropping, or OCR noise
- `Low confidence`: useful lead only; do not present as settled fact without cleaner evidence

## Iris Goodman / Iris Greenidge family narrative

The strongest narrative material in the ZIPs concerns `Iris Goodman`, later identified in one source as `Iris Greenidge`.

### High-confidence points

- A caption clearly identifies:
  - `IRIS GREENIDGE: wants a closure to the mystery surrounding her father's disappearance.`

- Iris was born on `June 30, 1913`.

- Her birthplace is given as `Hillaby, St. Andrew`.

- She attended `Turners Hall Primary School`.

- She later attended `St. Simon's Girls School`.

- She was sent to `Queen's College` in `1926`.

- She boarded on `Ocean View Road, off Spooners Hill`, with her father's cousin `Mrs. Agard`.

- She later taught at `St. Andrew's Girls' School`.

### Medium-confidence points

- The article says the Hillaby family included `seven children`, described as `two girls and five boys`.

- The father at the center of the disappearance story appears to be `Archie Goodman` / `Archibald Goodman`.

- The article suggests the family never received proper closure on what happened to him.

- Iris recalled her father as kind and considerate, and said he bought her a bicycle.

- The eldest brother `Frank` was about `19` at the time of the disappearance and taught at `Holy Innocents Boys' School`.

### Low-confidence but important leads

The article OCR also suggests:

- rumours linked the father's disappearance to events around St. Andrew
- references to `Turners Hill`
- names such as `Mr. Gill` and `Reid`
- a belief within the family that foul play may have been involved

These details are worth preserving as family research leads, but should be treated as unresolved historical memory unless corroborated by clearer source copies.
